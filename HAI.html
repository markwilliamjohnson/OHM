Surveillance systems compared

Conceptual alignment precedes numerical comparison.

England — UKHSA

Coverage
Mandatory surveillance for specified organisms and infections.
Native outputs
Case counts, rates per population, rates per bed-days, onset classifications.
Time
Monthly data and April–March financial years.
Onset
For CDI annual reporting, hospital-onset is a positive specimen on day 3 or later, with admission as day 1.
Scope note
This embedded “UK” comparator is England, not a pooled four-nation UK series.

United States — CDC/NHSN

Coverage
Participating acute-care hospitals reporting to NHSN; reporting requirements vary by programme and measure.
Native outputs
Observed events, model-predicted events and risk-adjusted SIR.
Time
Calendar years in the embedded annual progress-report snapshots.
Onset
Hospital-onset MRSA and CDI are detected on day 4 or later after admission.
Baseline
The 2024 progress report uses the 2015 national baseline for these SIR comparisons.
