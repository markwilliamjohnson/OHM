From physical constraint to symbiogenesis

A visual argument: life does not escape physics. It recruits, balances, and progressively organises physical constraints.
1 · Amphiphiles in water
Physical chemistry
Water makes exposed hydrocarbon tails costly.
Amphiphilic molecules move randomly. Their hydrophilic heads remain comfortable in water, while hydrophobic tails tend to reduce contact with it.

Interpretive thread

This is not a literal evolutionary history. It is an explanatory sequence linking self-assembly, constraint, biological organisation and symbiosis.

1. Physical regularityThermodynamics supplies tendencies before there is life.
2. Hydrophobic aggregationTails cluster to reduce energetically costly contact with water.
3. Negative charge as counter-constraintCharged headgroups repel one another and help prevent unlimited collapse.
4. Gravity: analogy, not micelle mechanismGravity shapes macroscopic environments, but molecular assembly is dominated by electromagnetic and thermodynamic interactions.
5. Boundary becomes functionalA stable compartment can maintain gradients and couple processes.
6. Constraint regulates constraintChannels, gradients and metabolism make the boundary conditional and active.
7. SymbiogenesisPreviously independent organisms become mutually constraining parts of a larger persistent system.
