What this visualisation assumes

The exact model is a two-dimensional vector space over 𝔽₃. A nonzero square-zero operator is represented, after a change of basis, by N = [[0,s],[0,0]]. It sends the second basis direction to the first and sends the first to zero.

The trines are I, I+N, and I−N. They form a cyclic group of order three. An anti-trine such as A = −I+N has A³ = −I and A⁶ = I. Its powers alternate between anti-trines and trines, producing the six-node orbit shown in the app.

The lattice drawing uses the signed representatives −1, 0, and 1 for the residue classes 2, 0, and 1. Apparent jumps across the drawing are modular wrap-around, not discontinuities in the finite geometry.

The “real comparison” deliberately keeps the same matrix while changing the arithmetic. There, N² may still be zero, but (I+N)³ = I+3N rather than I. This makes the role of characteristic three visible rather than treating the cubic identity as an ordinary real-space fact.
