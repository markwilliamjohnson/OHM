When to get extra support

The app is not a substitute for healthcare or human contact

Consider speaking to a GP, physiotherapist or registered dietitian if breathlessness, chest discomfort, joint pain, very low mood, binge eating, sleep problems or mobility difficulties are persistent.

For loneliness, a low-pressure first step might be a regular café, walking group, volunteering, community class, online interest group or a recurring call with one person.

Seek urgent medical help for severe chest pain, sudden breathing difficulty, fainting, or other acute symptoms.

Design principle

Small Steps avoids punishment, shame and all-or-nothing goals. It is designed to help a person notice patterns, create tiny interruptions in automatic habits, increase movement gradually, and make social contact easier.
